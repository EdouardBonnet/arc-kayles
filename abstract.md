Arc Kayles is the normal-play game in which two players alternately remove
the endpoints of an edge of a finite undirected graph. We state its
$\mathrm{PSPACE}$-completeness under polynomial-time many-one reductions.
The reduction is from the positive CNF game. Its main structural ingredient
is the Sprague–Grundy formula
$g(a,b)=(a+b)\bmod 2+2(\min(a,b)\bmod 2)$ for a biclique $K_{a,b}$
with an independent set of additional vertices, where every biclique vertex
has a pendant neighbor in that independent set.
